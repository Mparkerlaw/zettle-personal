How to host it

  1. Pick a round and read the call-out — “This round is for any moms!” — and wave them up.
  2. Ask the icebreaker so everyone hears what they've been up to since graduation.
  3. Type their names, then questions rotate between contestants. Tap the year they shout out.
  4. Add your own teacher / prom / homecoming questions in the 📝 Class Insiders round before the party.
  5. Points follow people across rounds. Finish with the ⚡ Lightning Round (double points), then hit Awards to crown the champion. 🏆
