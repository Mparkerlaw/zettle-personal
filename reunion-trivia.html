How to host it

  1. Pick a tile and read the call-out — each category is locked to its group (“MOMS & PARENTS-TO-BE ONLY!”), so only they can come up and answer it. Knights Pride, Guess Who, and Lightning are open to everyone.
  2. Read the question. They shout a year, you tap it. Then tap who answered to give them the points — names are added on the fly.
  3. Board 2 is double points. When the boards run dry, hit ⚡ Lightning for 1,000-point bonus questions.
  4. Before the party: load yearbook photos into 🖼️ Guess Who (the ✎ on its column) and write teacher/prom questions in 📝 Class Insiders.
  5. End with 🏆 Awards — the top score gets the championship certificate.
